## Pro Agreement

Last updated: November 19, 2026

### Summary

This summary is here to help you read the agreement. It is not part of the agreement, and if it differs from the sections below, the sections control.

- You pay only for leads you choose to claim or buy. Every price is shown before you buy.
- A lead is an introduction, not a job. We do not promise that an owner will answer, hire you or pay you.
- A lead may be sold to as many as 5 pros unless you buy it exclusive.
- Purchases are final, except that a bad lead gets a credit or a refund under the Lead Credit Policy.
- You must be verified, and keep your insurance current, to claim or buy leads.
- You may use an owner's details only to contact that owner about that job.
- Membership is free for your first year. From the second year it renews automatically at $300 a year on your card on file, until you close your account. We email you before each charge.
- Replying YES to a lead we text you is an order. The price comes out of your wallet, and if the wallet is short your card on file is charged a reload.
- Disputes go to individual arbitration in Tennessee. You can opt out within 30 days.

### 1. This agreement

This Pro Agreement (the "Agreement") is between you, the business creating a pro account, and Dalton Enterprises LLC, doing business as Hammer Hound, \[Address\] ("Hammer Hound", "we", "us"). It governs your pro account and every lead you view, claim or buy.

The Terms of Use, Privacy Policy and SMS Policy are part of this Agreement. If this Agreement and the Terms of Use conflict about a pro account, this Agreement controls.

You accept this Agreement by ticking the box and creating your account. That has the same effect as signing it. We keep a record of the version you accepted, the time and the internet address used.

The person who accepts this Agreement confirms that they are at least 18 and have authority to bind the business. "You" includes the business and its owners, employees, agents and subcontractors.

### 2. What we do

**2.1 The service.** We advertise to property owners, collect their requests for work and offer those requests to pros as leads. An offer shows the trade, the area, a description of the job and the price. It does not show the owner's name, phone number or address. Those are released to you only when you claim or buy the lead.

**2.2 Who else gets a lead.** A lead may be sold to as many pros as the property owner allowed, up to 5. If no other pro has bought a lead, you may buy it at the exclusive price shown, and we will not sell it to anyone else.

**2.3 What a lead is not.** A lead is an introduction. We do not promise that a property owner will answer, will want a quote, will hire you or will pay you. We do not promise any number of leads, any volume of work or any return on what you spend.

**2.4 Where leads come from.** Leads come from our own websites and advertising. We take reasonable steps to confirm that a request is real, but we do not guarantee the accuracy of what a property owner tells us about themselves, the property or the job.

**2.5 We are not a party to your work.** Any contract for work is between you and the property owner. We are not a contractor, employer, agent, partner or insurer for either of you, and we do not supervise, direct or inspect your work.

### 3. What you agree to

**3.1 Accurate information.** Everything you give us is true, current and yours to give: your business name, legal name, Employer Identification Number, trades, service area, licenses and insurance. You will update it when it changes.

**3.2 Licenses, insurance and the law.** You hold, and will keep, every license, permit, registration, bond and insurance policy that the law requires for the work you do where you do it. You will follow all laws that apply to your business, including consumer protection, home improvement, contracting, advertising and tax laws.

**3.3 Using an owner's information.** You will use a lead only to contact that property owner about that request. You will not sell, share, rent or reuse it, add it to a marketing list, or pass it to another business.

**3.4 Calls and texts.** You are solely responsible for how you contact a property owner. You will follow the Telephone Consumer Protection Act, the Telemarketing Sales Rule, do-not-call rules and state law. You will stop contacting an owner who asks you to stop.

**3.5 Your conduct.** You will deal honestly and respectfully with property owners. You will not misrepresent your qualifications, pressure an owner, or discriminate against anyone on a basis the law protects.

**3.6 Your account.** Your account is for your business only. You are responsible for everything done through it and for keeping your sign-in details secure. One business may hold one account. We accept one account per Employer Identification Number.

**3.7 What you may not do.** You may not create an account with false information, open more than one account to obtain welcome credit, resell or share leads, copy or scrape the service, interfere with it, or use it to break any law.

### 4. Verification and the Verified Pro badge

**4.1 Viewing before verification.** You may create an account and view available leads before you are verified. You cannot claim, buy or receive a lead, including with welcome credit, until you are verified.

**4.2 What we ask for.** To be verified you give us your legal business name and Employer Identification Number and upload one proof of your business and proof of insurance. We do not accept a Social Security number in place of an Employer Identification Number.

**4.3 How we check.** We review your documents with automated tools, including artificial intelligence, and may ask for more. You may ask for a person to review a result.

**4.4 What "Verified" means.** "Verified" and the Verified Pro badge mean only that we received documents that appeared valid on the date we reviewed them. They are not a license check, a background check, an endorsement or a guarantee of your work. You will not describe them as any of those.

**4.5 Expiry and removal.** Verification ends on the date the insurance on file expires, until you upload a current certificate. We may remove verification if a document was false, altered or no longer valid. When verification ends or is removed, you must stop displaying the badge.

**4.6 Using the badge.** While you are verified, you may display the Verified Pro badge on your own website, vehicles and printed materials, unaltered, to say that you are a Hammer Hound Verified Pro. We own the badge and the Hammer Hound name and marks. This permission ends when your verification or your account ends.

### 5. Fees and payment

**5.1 Lead prices.** The price of each lead is shown in the offer before you buy. Prices vary by trade, job, market and the age of the lead, and may change at any time for leads you have not yet bought.

**5.2 Wallet and card on file.** Leads are paid from your wallet. You add to your wallet with a card, and you may save that card on file. A wallet balance is not a bank deposit and earns no interest.

**5.2.1 Claiming by text.** A lead offer we text you states the job and the price. Replying YES, or ONLY for an exclusive lead, is an order for that lead. The price is taken from your wallet. If your wallet does not cover it and you have a card on file, you authorize us to charge that card a reload, in the reload amount set in your wallet, and to take the price from the reloaded wallet. The reply gives you the lead's details. Every card charge is confirmed by an emailed receipt.

**5.2.2 Claiming in your account.** If your wallet does not cover a lead, the claim button states the reload amount and the card before you press it. Pressing it authorizes that charge.

**5.2.3 Automatic reload.** If you turn on automatic reload, you authorize us to charge your card on file the reload amount you chose whenever your wallet cannot cover the price of a lead, up to any monthly limit you set. You can change the amount, set a limit or turn it off at any time in your wallet. We email a receipt for every charge.

**5.2.4 Topping up by text.** We may text you when your wallet is low, with a choice of amounts. Replying with a choice, or texting TOPUP with an amount, authorizes us to charge that amount to your card on file and add it to your wallet. We email a receipt.

**5.2.5 Invoices.** We email an invoice each month, as a PDF, listing every charge, lead and credit. Your invoices are also in your wallet.

**5.3 Welcome credit.** A new pro account starts with $240 of welcome credit, which is enough for 3 shared leads at the standard price of $75. Welcome credit can be spent only on leads, only after you pass verification, and is spent before money you add. It has no cash value, cannot be withdrawn, transferred or refunded, and may be changed or ended for new accounts at any time. If a lead paid for with welcome credit qualifies under the Lead Credit Policy, the welcome credit is returned to your balance; it is not paid out.

**5.4 All purchases are final** except as stated in the Lead Credit Policy in section 6.

**5.5 Annual membership and automatic renewal.** A pro account carries an annual membership fee of $300. Membership is free for your first year, counted from the day you create your account. On the first anniversary of that day, and on each anniversary after it, the fee of $300 is charged automatically to your card on file and covers the next 12 months. It renews every year until you close your account. We email you a renewal notice about 35 days, 18 days and 3 days before each charge. Each notice states the amount, the date, that the membership renews until you cancel, and how to cancel. To cancel, close your account from the Account page of your portal or by replying to any email from us; it takes effect at once and there is no fee. If you cancel before a renewal date you are not charged for the next year. We email you a written confirmation of these terms when you create your account. If the charge fails, we may pause your ability to claim leads until it is paid. \[Membership fees are not refundable once charged.\] The membership fee is separate from the price of leads and is not paid from welcome credit.

**5.6 Taxes.** Prices do not include taxes. You are responsible for any taxes on what you buy, other than taxes on our income.

**5.7 Chargebacks.** If you dispute a charge with your card issuer instead of using the Lead Credit Policy, we may suspend your account while the dispute is open.

### 6. Lead Credit Policy

**6.1 What qualifies.** You may ask for a credit if a lead you paid for had a wrong or disconnected phone number, was a duplicate of a lead you already bought from us, was not a real request for service, or was outside the trades or service area in your account when you bought it.

**6.2 What does not qualify.** The owner did not answer, hired someone else, changed their mind, or the job was smaller than described.

**6.3 How to ask.** Report the lead from your account or through support within \[7\] days of purchase.

**6.4 How we decide.** We review our records of the request using automated tools, including artificial intelligence. You may ask for a person to review the result. We aim to decide within \[3\] business days.

**6.5 Credit or refund.** For each approved request you choose a credit to your balance or a refund to your card on file. Refunds reach your card in about \[3 to 5\] business days.

**6.6 Abuse.** We may limit or refuse credits for an account that reports leads in bad faith.
