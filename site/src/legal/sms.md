## SMS Policy

Last updated: November 19, 2026

Hammer Hound, operated by Dalton Enterprises LLC, runs two text-message programs. This policy covers both.

### 1. Property owner request messages

**What you get.** Texts about a request you submitted: a confirmation, a notice that we are calling to confirm, the names of Pros who accepted, and replies from support. Pros who take your request may also text you about it.

**How you opt in.** By entering your mobile number and agreeing to the consent statement when you submit a request.

**How often.** Message frequency varies with your request, usually a few messages per request.

### 2. Pro lead offers

**What you get.** A text each time a lead matching your trades and service area is available. The offer shows the trade, area, job size and price, but not the owner's contact details. If you buy, a second text delivers the full details.

**How you opt in.** By ticking "Text me lead offers" when you sign up, or turning on "Text me new offers" in your account.

**How often.** Recurring messages. Frequency varies with demand in your area and may be several a day.

**Buying by text.** Replying YES buys the lead at the shared price shown. Replying ONLY buys it at the exclusive price shown, if no one else has bought. Your reply is your order and authorizes us to deduct that price from your balance. Offers end at the time stated or when all places are taken.

**Quiet hours.** If you set quiet hours in your account, we hold offers during those hours.

### 3. Terms for both programs

- **Cost.** We do not charge for texts. Message and data rates may apply from your carrier.
- **Stop.** Reply STOP to any message to stop texts from that program. We send one message confirming the opt-out. Pros who stop lead offers can still buy leads in their account.
- **Help.** Reply HELP, or use the Contact page.
- **Consent is not a condition of purchase.** You do not have to agree to texts to use Hammer Hound, though a property owner request cannot be completed without a phone number Pros can reach.
- **Automated messages.** Messages may be sent using automated technology.
- **Carriers.** Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages. Delivery is not guaranteed.
- **Changing numbers.** If you change or give up your mobile number, update your account or tell us so we stop texting the old number.
- **Privacy.** We do not share mobile numbers, text-message opt-in data or consent with third parties or affiliates for their marketing. See the Privacy Policy.
- **Eligibility.** You must be 18 or older and the account holder or authorized user of the number.
