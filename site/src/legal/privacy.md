## Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 19, 2026

### 1. Scope

This policy explains what Dalton Enterprises LLC ("Hammer Hound", "we") collects when you use our websites, calls, text messages and related services, how we use it, and who receives it. It applies to property owners, Pros and visitors.

### 2. The short version

- If you submit a request, your name, phone number, address and job details go to the Pros who take your request, up to the number you chose (1 to 5). That is the service you asked for.
- We call you with an AI assistant to confirm your request, and we record and transcribe that call.
- We do not sell your information to data brokers or marketing lists.
- You can ask for a copy of your information or ask us to delete it from the Contact page.

### 3. What we collect

| Category | Examples | From |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Contact details | Name, mobile number, email, job or business address | You |
| Request details | Trade, job description, size, budget, timing, property type | You, and your confirmation call |
| Call and message records | Recordings and transcripts of calls with our AI assistant, text messages with us, call times and outcomes | Your calls and texts with us |
| Pro business records | Business name, legal business name, Employer Identification Number (EIN), trades, service area, proof-of-business documents, insurance certificates and their expiry date | The Pro |
| Payment details | Card type and last four digits, charges, balance history. Full card numbers are held by our payment processor, not by us. | The Pro, our payment processor |
| Device and usage | IP address, browser, pages viewed, approximate location, cookies | Your device |
| Support records | Chat messages, tickets, dispute decisions | You |

### 4. How we use it

- To confirm requests and send them to Pros.
- To offer leads to Pros, take payment and manage balances.
- To check Pro documents and decide lead-credit requests.
- To prevent fraud and fake requests, and to keep the Service secure.
- To answer support questions and meet legal obligations.
- To measure and improve the Service and our advertising.

### 5. How we use AI

We use automated tools, including AI, to make the confirmation call, transcribe it, score whether a lead was a real and reachable request, review documents Pros upload, answer support chats and draft cost guides. You may ask for a person to review a decision that affects you, such as a declined verification or a lead-credit decision. Cost guides do not contain personal information.

### 6. Who receives it

- **Pros.** The Pros who take a property owner's request receive that owner's contact and job details. Pros pay us for this introduction. They may use the information only to contact the owner about that request. \[Some state laws may treat this as a "sale" of personal information. You can opt out by not submitting a request, by stopping further calls from your status page, or by contacting us.\]
- **Property owners.** A property owner sees the business name of each Pro who accepted their request.
- **Service providers.** Companies that work for us under contract: telephone and text carriers, voice AI and transcription, payment processing, hosting, database, email, analytics and advertising measurement.
- **Legal and safety.** When required by law or needed to protect rights, safety or the Service.
- **Business transfers.** If the business is sold or merged, records may transfer to the new owner under this policy.

We do not share mobile numbers, text-message opt-in data or consent records with third parties or affiliates for their own marketing.

### 7. Call recording

Calls with our AI assistant are recorded and transcribed. The assistant says so at the start of the call. If you do not want to be recorded, end the call and use support chat instead.

### 8. Cookies and advertising

We use cookies and similar tools to keep the site working, remember your progress in a request, and measure which ads bring visitors. You can block cookies in your browser; parts of the site may not work.

### 10. Your choices and rights

- **Stop calls and texts.** Reply STOP to any text, use your status page, or tell any caller to stop.
- **Access, correct, delete.** Ask from the Contact page by choosing "Privacy or my data". We verify the request using the phone number or email on file and answer within \[45\] days.
- **State rights.** Depending on where you live, you may have rights to know, delete, correct, opt out of sale or targeted advertising, and appeal a refusal. We do not discriminate against anyone for using these rights.
- **What we cannot undo.** Information already delivered to a Pro at your request is held by that Pro. We will tell the Pro you asked for deletion.

### 11. Security

We use access controls, encryption in transit and vendor contracts to protect information. No system is perfectly secure.

### 12. Children

The Service is for adults. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.

### 13. Changes

We will post updates here with a new date and notify account holders of material changes.
